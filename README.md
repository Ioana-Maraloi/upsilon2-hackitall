# ⏳ A 3D Adventure Through History
HackITAll 2025 – GameJam Submission
Theme: Time Glitch
Built with Unity 3D & C#

Upsilon is a 3D third-person adventure game created in just 48 hours during the HackITAll 2025 GameJam. The theme of the event was "Time Glitch", which inspired us to craft a story-rich experience where the main character travels through distorted moments in history, completing challenges to restore the timeline and return to the present.

## 🧠 Story & Gameplay Concept
The game begins in the present day. You play as a man who just got home from work and lies down to rest in his apartment. Suddenly, a strange clock in the corner of the room starts spinning rapidly, accelerating time unnaturally. He falls asleep — and wakes up in a dark dungeon, where he meets a mysterious sorceress.

She informs him that he's a prisoner trapped in the past, sent back in time due to a temporal glitch. To return to the present, he must pass several trials across different historical eras. Each level completed moves him one step closer to modern time.


## 🌍 Levels & Challenges
🏛 Ancient Greece – Exploration & Collection
The first level is set in a 3D world inspired by Ancient Greece, featuring stone columns and a vast environment.

The player's task is to collect at least 7 out of 10 gems scattered across the map.

Once enough gems are gathered, a magical time portal appears and allows progression to the next era.

## 🏰 The Middle Ages – Combat Trial
The second level places the player in front of a medieval castle, where enemy soldiers are patrolling.

The player picks up a sword and must engage in close-combat.

Defeating at least half of the enemies opens the next time portal to move forward in time.
